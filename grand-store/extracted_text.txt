37. IF I WERE BUILDING GRAND STORE, THESE WOULD BE MY TOP 10 PRIORITIES
1. Build the website architecture around SEO from day one.
2. Create the Wine & Spirits Knowledge Centre.
3. Build the Wine Estate directory.
4. Build Global Wines country → region → winery pages.
5. Give every genuine product, event, auction and winery its own optimised page.
6. Build original Grand Store content rather than copying supplier descriptions.
7. Build useful calculators, finders and interactive tools.
8. Develop genuine industry partnerships and high-quality backlinks.
9. Make technical SEO and page speed part of the developer's acceptance criteria.
10. Measure SEO by leads, bookings and revenue—not just rankings or traffic.
The biggest strategic opportunity is that Grand Store doesn't have to compete with ordinary liquor stores for the same keywords. You can build authority across wine education + wine tourism + international wineries + events + auctions + whisky + trade/import-export. Those areas can all link into one another, giving Google—and customers—a very clear picture that Grand Store is a comprehensive wine and spirits platform rather than simply another online bottle shop.
