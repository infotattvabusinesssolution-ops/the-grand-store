1. Mobile Number + OTP — probably your best option
Customer enters:
Mobile Number+27 82 123 4567
Then:
Enter 6-digit OTP
No password to remember.
After verification, the platform can ask for:
First name 
Last name 
Email 
Date of birth / age confirmation 
Delivery address 
This is particularly good for a platform such as Grand Store, where customers may be purchasing from their phones.
2. Google / Apple Sign-In
Have buttons such as:
Continue with GoogleContinue with Apple
The customer doesn't have to create another password.
However, after social sign-in, your platform should still collect whatever information is required for age verification and alcohol-sale compliance before allowing an alcohol purchase.
3. Email + Password
Traditional option:
Email addressPasswordSign In
Include:
Forgot Password?
This is useful for customers who prefer a conventional account and particularly useful for business/trade customers.
4. Email Magic Link
Customer enters their email:
customer@email.com
Then:
We've sent you a secure sign-in link.
They click the link in their email and are logged in.
This removes password frustration and is very user-friendly.
5. WhatsApp Login / OTP
For a South African-focused platform, this could be a very attractive option.
Continue with WhatsApp
Customer receives a verification code through WhatsApp.
It can also eventually be useful for:
Order notifications 
Delivery updates 
Auction notifications 
Promotional messages 
Abandoned-cart reminders 
6. Guest Checkout
I would definitely consider guest checkout, rather than forcing somebody to create an account before buying.
For example:
Add to Cart → Checkout → Continue as Guest
Then collect the minimum required information.
After the order:
Create an account to track your order, save your details and earn rewards.
This converts a guest buyer into a registered customer without creating friction.

Very important: separate LOGIN from AGE VERIFICATION
For alcohol, I wouldn't make the sign-in system itself responsible for everything.
Think of it as two separate gates:
1. Account authentication“Are you really this customer?”
2. Alcohol eligibility verification“Is this customer legally permitted to purchase alcohol?”
That allows you to have a very easy login while still having appropriate compliance controls.
For example:
Sign in → Verify age → Shop → Checkout → Delivery verification
Depending on the jurisdiction, you can make the age-verification process more or less stringent.

One more thing I would add
Since your platform has normal retail + premium whisky + auctions + potentially wholesale/trade, don't make every customer account identical.
Use a single Customer ID, but allow account types:
Retail Customer↓Premium Customer↓Verified Auction Bidder↓Trade Customer / Business↓Vendor
That gives you one central identity system while allowing different verification levels and permissions.
For an online liquor + premium whisky auction platform, my preferred combination would be:
Mobile OTP + Google + Apple + Email + Guest Checkout
with age verification at the appropriate point and additional verification for auction bidders.
