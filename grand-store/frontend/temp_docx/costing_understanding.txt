Vendor Pricing Explanation
How Your Product Price Is Calculated
When you complete the product pricing form, the system will clearly show you how your product will be priced and what price the customer will see on the platform.
You simply enter your cost/price and the percentage you would like to add for your own profit.
The platform will then automatically calculate the final selling price.
Example
If your product cost is:
R100.00
You decide to add:
20% profit
Your price becomes:
R120.00
The platform will then apply its required platform margin of 15% and calculate the final customer selling price.
The important point is that the platform's minimum 15% margin is protected automatically. You do not need to calculate this yourself.
What You Will See
The form will clearly show:

Therefore, you can see exactly:
What your original cost is
What profit percentage you have added
What amount you will receive

What the customer will see as the selling price
The Purpose
The system is designed to make the calculation simple, transparent and easy to understand.
You decide your own profit percentage.
The platform then calculates the customer-facing price while ensuring that the platform receives not less than its required 15% margin.
You therefore do not need to manually work out complicated pricing formulas.
Promotions and Special Offers
If the platform later runs a promotion, coupon, Refer & Earn campaign or other customer incentive, the system will calculate this separately.
Your agreed product price and profit will remain clearly identifiable, while the platform can manage its own promotional costs and incentives.
Important
The price displayed to the customer may therefore be higher than the price you enter, because it includes the platform's required margin and any applicable charges.
Everything is shown clearly before the product is submitted, so you can see the exact figures and understand the final customer selling price.
