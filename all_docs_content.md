Absolutely. For Grand Store, I would make the courier/shipping process a completely transparent part of the buying journey, especially because you will have vendors shipping directly to customers and potentially selling to different countries.
The customer should never reach the final payment page and suddenly discover an unexpected courier charge.
Below is the structure I recommend giving your developer.
GRAND STORE — COURIER & DELIVERY SYSTEM
Simple. Transparent. Trackable.
The product price and delivery cost are shown separately before you pay.
Delivery charges are calculated according to the vendor's location, customer's delivery country/area, product quantity/weight, package dimensions and selected delivery service.
