# Asset provenance

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project. Optimized WebP files are served by the site; the original generated PNG files are retained alongside them.

Smaller `-sm.webp` variants are generated by `scripts/optimize-assets.mjs` and selected automatically using responsive image markup. These retain the artwork and product photography without visual alterations.

## Cape Winelands editorial artwork

- Site asset: `public/assets/cape-wineland.webp`
- Original: `public/assets/cape-wineland.png`
- Used in: brand story, open graph image.
- Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-width editorial vineyard photograph for the GrandStore luxury wine and spirits landing page. Primary request: create an exquisite cinematic photograph of the South African Cape Winelands at late afternoon, Stellenbosch mountain ranges rising behind gently rolling vineyard rows, a small classic Cape Dutch white gabled wine estate nestled in the middle distance, warm sunshine over deep olive grape vines, atmospheric layered distant mountains. Composition: panoramic landscape 16:9, a slightly elevated viewpoint, foreground vineyards with leading lines sweeping toward the right, mountains across the upper half, expansive and quiet, no people close up. Style: natural analog editorial landscape photography, authentic landscape textures, subtle film grain, deep natural greens, hazy pale stone sky, sophisticated wine magazine art direction. Not oversaturated. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no logos. This is atmospheric editorial artwork, not a claim to depict a specific estate.

## Existing GrandStore assets

- `public/assets/logo.webp`: unchanged source logo from the existing GrandStore frontend.
- `public/assets/brand-logo.webp`: a smaller, optimized copy for the header and footer, with the original proportions and transparent background preserved.
- `public/assets/products/*.webp`: twelve product photographs from GrandStore's public catalog. Each source URL, product ID, name, country, and destination is retained in `src/catalog.json`.
- Catalog source: https://api.grandstoreglobal.com/api/products
- Collected: 2026-09-14. Price and availability are intentionally left to the main store.

## Code-native artwork

The interactive hero bottles are original Three.js lathed meshes with canvas label textures and generated studio lighting. Their labels were adapted to the user's requested dark and gold GrandStore theme. They are illustrative brand artwork. Product cards use catalog photos rather than generated replicas.

Fonts are locally bundled through Fontsource (Cormorant Garamond and Manrope). Icons are Phosphor Icons. Font and icon licenses are included in their respective dependency packages.
